# IntelliSphere Travel and Expense Policy

## Travel Eligibility

Employees may claim reimbursement for approved business travel.

## Air Travel

Economy class airfare is reimbursable.

Business class requires Director approval.

## Hotel Stays

Employees may claim up to $250 per night.

## Meals

Daily meal allowance is $75.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle usage is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile.

## Expense Submission

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of trip completion.

## Required Documentation

Receipts are required for any expense above $25.
