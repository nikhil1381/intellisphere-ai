# IntelliSphere IT Support Guide

## Password Resets

Employees should contact the IT Help Desk or use self-service password reset.

## VPN Access

VPN requests require manager approval.

## Laptop Requests

Employees can request a laptop through the Service Portal.

## Software Requests

Software installations require approval from IT Security.

## Support Hours

Support is available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
